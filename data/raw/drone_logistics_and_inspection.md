# Unmanned Logistics & Infrastructure Inspection Whitepaper (India)

## 1. Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) Medical Logistics
India's hilly and forested terrains in states like Arunachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and Himachal Pradesh present severe transport bottlenecks for critical healthcare.

### ICMR 'i-Drone' Pilot Program:
- **Mission**: Transporting anti-snake venom, COVID-19 vaccines, and emergency blood bags from regional hubs to remote primary health centers (PHCs).
- **Performance**: Reduced transit duration between Bishnupur and Karang island in Manipur from 4 hours via ferry boat to 12 minutes using hybrid VTOL fixed-wing drones.
- **Payload Specifications**: Cold-chain payload box maintaining 2°C to 8°C internal temperature with active temperature monitoring telemetry.

## 2. Infrastructure & Energy Sector Applications

### Power Grid Inspection:
State power utilities (like PowerGrid Corporation of India) utilize thermal-equipped micro drones to inspect 400kV high-voltage transmission towers. Drones identify overheating insulators and hairline structural cracks without de-energizing lines, preventing massive grid failures.

### Oil & Gas Pipeline Monitoring:
GAIL and Indian Oil Corporation deploy long-range surveillance drones to monitor thousands of kilometers of underground natural gas pipelines, detecting illegal encroachment, vegetation overgrowth, and micro-leaks via optical gas imaging (OGI) payloads.

### Solar PV Plant Maintenance:
Solar farms in Rajasthan and Gujarat deploy automated drone mapping to survey 100MW solar plants in 2 hours instead of 3 weeks of manual inspection. Radiometric thermal cameras detect defective solar cells and micro-cracks instantly.
