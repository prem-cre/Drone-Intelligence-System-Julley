# Case Study: Agricultural Drone Spraying & Economics in India

## Executive Summary
Precision agriculture powered by agricultural drones (such as IoTechWorld Agribot, Marut AG30, and Garuda Kisan Drone) is transforming Indian farming across cotton, paddy, sugarcane, and chilli belts in Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Punjab, and Maharashtra.

---

## 1. Field Performance Comparison: Manual vs Drone Spraying

| Parameter | Traditional Manual Spraying | Drone Spraying (Small Category) | Improvement / Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Water Consumption** | 200 - 250 Liters / Acre | 10 - 12 Liters / Acre | **95% reduction in water** |
| **Pesticide Consumption** | 100% standard dosage | 70% - 80% ultra-low volume | **20% - 30% chemical savings** |
| **Time per Acre** | 3.5 - 4.0 Hours | 7 - 10 Minutes | **25x faster execution** |
| **Daily Coverage** | 2 - 3 Acres per laborer | 25 - 35 Acres per operator | **10x higher throughput** |
| **Health Impact** | High dermal/respiratory risk | Zero direct chemical contact | **100% health safety** |

## 2. Economic Analysis & Entrepreneurial ROI Model

### Initial Investment (10L Agriculture Drone Setup):
- **Drone Unit Price (DGCA Type Certified)**: ₹6,50,000
- **Extra Batteries (4 Sets) + Fast Charger**: ₹1,20,000
- **Pilot Training (RPC Fee at RPTO)**: ₹35,000
- **Total Initial Outlay**: ₹8,05,000 *(Can be reduced to ₹3,50,000 with 50-80% Subsidies under AIF / SMAM / Namo Drone Didi Scheme)*.

### Monthly Financial Operating Model:
- **Average Spraying Service Fee**: ₹450 per acre
- **Target Monthly Coverage**: 500 acres (20 days @ 25 acres/day)
- **Gross Monthly Revenue**: ₹2,25,000 (500 acres × ₹450)
- **Direct Operating Expenses**:
  - Battery degradation & electricity charging: ₹25,000
  - Vehicle transport & field logistics: ₹20,000
  - Pilot & Helper allowance: ₹35,000
  - Maintenance & insurance reserve: ₹15,000
- **Net Monthly Operating Profit**: ₹1,30,000

### Payback & Break-Even Timeline:
- **Without Subsidy**: Break-even achieved in **6.2 months**.
- **With 50% Government Subsidy**: Break-even achieved in **2.8 months**.
- **Net 3-Year Profit Projection**: Exceeds ₹35,00,000.

## 3. Agronomic Benefits & Crop Health Surveying
Multispectral sensors mounted on survey drones compute Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) heatmaps. This enables farmers to detect nitrogen deficiency, fungal rust, and pest infestation 14 days before visible leaf discoloration occurs.
